UGC for UAE brands: briefs, rights and what to check

UGC, short videos made by creators in the style of a real customer, often outperforms polished brand video in ads. Getting good UGC comes down to a clear brief and clear rights.

UGC versus influencer marketing

The two get confused because the same people often do both. The difference is what you're buying. With influencer marketing you pay for access to an audience. With UGC you pay for the content itself, delivered as files, which you then run on your own channels and in your ads.

Side by side

Two different jobs

Influencer marketing

  • Posted on the creator's account
  • You pay for their audience
  • Reach and trust from followers

UGC

  • Delivered to you as files
  • You pay for the content
  • Used in your ads and on your channels

Why UGC works in ads

Ads that look like ads get scrolled past. A person talking to camera about a product, filmed on a phone, looks like the content around it in the feed, so people give it a few seconds more. It also lets you test many angles cheaply: different hooks, different people, different benefits. That testing is where most of the value is.

Writing a creator brief

Most disappointing UGC comes from a vague brief. Creators aren't mind readers, and "make it authentic" isn't direction. A one-page brief with these sections is usually enough.

A one-page UGC brief

SectionWhat to include
Product and audienceWhat it is, who buys it, the one problem it solves
Key messageOne message per video. Not three
Hooks to tryTwo or three opening lines or visual ideas
Must includeProduct shown in use, brand name said clearly
Must avoidClaims you can't make, competitor names, pricing that may change
FormatVertical, length, captions, raw files or edited
Deadline and revisionsDelivery date and how many rounds of changes

Rights and contracts

This is where brands get caught out. Paying for a video doesn't automatically mean you can use it anywhere, forever. Agree the following before you pay, in writing.

  1. Where you can use the content: organic social, paid ads, website, email, in-store screens.
  2. For how long, and in which countries.
  3. Whether you can edit it, cut it into versions or combine it with other footage.
  4. Whether you can run it as an ad from the creator's own account (often called whitelisting or partnership ads), which is a separate permission.
  5. Who owns the raw footage.

How UGC is usually priced

Creators price in different ways, so compare like with like. Some charge per video, some sell packages of several videos, and many charge extra for raw footage, extra hooks or paid usage rights. Ask what's included before you compare quotes: number of videos, length, revisions, raw files and the usage period. A cheaper quote without paid usage rights can end up costing more.

Finding and testing creators

Social and content · Dubai

A low-risk way to start

Shortlist

Look for creators who already make the style you want and speak to your audience.

Small order

Order one or two videos from three or four creators with the same brief.

Run as ads

Test them side by side with the same budget and audience.

Reorder

Go back to the creators whose videos held attention and drove action.

For running the tests, see Meta ads in the UAE and TikTok ads in the UAE. If you'd rather produce some of it in-house, my short-form video system works for a team with a phone and an afternoon a week.

UAE-specific checks

If the creator also publishes the content on their own accounts as promotion, the UAE Media Council's advertiser permit rules may apply. See influencer marketing rules in the UAE. Reported guidance also says specialised ads, including real estate, healthcare, finance, education and food, need prior approval, so regulated brands should check claims and permits before anything is filmed.

Privacy is the other check. If a creator films in public or includes other people, those people need to have agreed. UAE law treats capturing or publishing someone's image without consent as an offence.

Common mistakes

  • Scripting every word, so the video sounds like an ad read by an actor.
  • Asking for one long video instead of several short variations.
  • Forgetting to agree paid usage rights before running the content as an ad.
  • Judging a creator on one video instead of a small test.

Test before scaling

Order a few videos from different creators, run them as ads, and reorder from the creators whose videos perform.
