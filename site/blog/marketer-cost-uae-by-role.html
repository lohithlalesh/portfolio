Hiring a marketer

What a marketer costs in the UAE, by role, and what each one contributes

"Marketer" is not one job. A social media executive, an SEO specialist and a head of marketing do different work and cost very different amounts. Here is what each role contributes, and what published UAE salary data says they cost.

When business owners ask me what a marketer costs, they usually mean one of two things: what would I pay a person on my payroll, or what would I pay someone I bring in from outside. Both questions only make sense once you know which role you are hiring for. The price follows the contribution, not the job title.

"Don't narrow it down to freelancers only. It's about the individual, and what they will actually be able to contribute." Lohith Lalesh

Where these numbers come from

The salary figures below come from the Hays GCC Salary Guide 2025, from its Marketing and Digital Marketing tables for the UAE. Hays states these are the total fixed monthly salary package in AED, as of Q4 2024, built from job listings, job offers and candidate registrations with Hays Middle East, with averages rounded to the nearest AED 500. Hays also notes that location, size of business, industry and experience all affect pay.

Two caveats. Recruiter data leans towards companies that use recruiters, so a five-person business may see a different market. And the data is from late 2024. The Cooper Fitch UAE Salary Guide 2026, as reported by Khaleej Times in November 2025, found employers planning increases of 1.6% to 4% for 2026, so treat the ranges as a starting point, not a quote.

What each role actually contributes

Social media marketer

A social media executive keeps the accounts alive: posts go out, comments and DMs get answered, and you get a monthly summary. A social media manager decides what to post and why, and connects it to sales. The jump between them on the Hays table (an average of AED 24,000 versus AED 30,500) is the price of judgement. If you already know what you want to say, the executive is enough. If you need someone to work that out, you need the manager. See what a social media manager in Dubai should deliver.

Content writer

A good writer gives you pages that rank, ads that get clicked and product copy that answers objections. Hays does not list a separate copywriter line for the UAE, so the content specialist band is the closest benchmark. What moves the price most is language: writing natively in Arabic and English is closer to two jobs than one. More on that in Arabic and English content.

Graphic designer and video editor

Designers turn a plan into something people stop scrolling for. The Hays range for a graphic or digital designer is wide (AED 15,000 to 30,000), which reflects the gap between someone producing templates and someone who can art-direct a campaign. Hays has no UAE line for video editors, so I can't give you a sourced salary. What drives the price is whether they only edit footage you supply, or also plan, shoot, animate and caption for each platform.

SEO specialist and performance marketer

Hays groups SEO, PPC and SEM together, at AED 18,000 to 24,000 for an executive and AED 25,000 to 40,000 for a manager. In practice these are different skills. An SEO specialist fixes the site, builds content and earns visibility over months. A performance marketer spends your ad budget and should be able to show cost per lead within weeks. Ask which one a candidate really is. A head of performance marketing sits at AED 45,000 to 65,000 in the same guide.

Marketing executive

This is the most common first hire, and the most misunderstood. An executive executes. Given a plan, they can run several channels competently. Without a plan, most will default to posting on social media because it is visible. That is a briefing problem, not a talent problem.

Marketing manager

A manager owns the plan, the budget and the result. They should be able to say which channel brings enquiries and what each one costs.

Fractional CMO or senior consultant

A full-time CMO in the Hays data averages AED 125,000 a month, which few small companies can justify. The alternative is buying senior judgement by the hour or day. Hays doesn't publish fractional rates, and I couldn't find a sourced UAE figure for part-time CMOs. The nearest published data is Upwork's 2026 guide, which puts marketing directors at $60 to $120 an hour on its platform. The price depends on days per month and whether they own results or only advise. See what a marketing consultant does.

Salary is not the whole cost

The figures above are salary packages. An employee also brings visa, insurance, leave and end-of-service gratuity costs. I break those down in in-house marketer or freelancer.

How I would use these numbers

Name the outcome first, then decide whether you need someone to execute a plan or someone to write it. That is usually the executive versus manager decision. If the budget fits an executive but the business needs a manager's judgement, a common answer is an executive plus a few senior hours each month. The digital marketing cost guide covers what services and packages cost, as opposed to people.
