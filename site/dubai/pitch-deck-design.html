Pitch deck and presentation design in Dubai

Investors and clients in Dubai see a lot of decks. The ones that work are short, make one point per slide and tell a story the room can repeat afterwards. I write the storyline and design the slides, so you walk in with something that sounds like you.

Story first, slides second

Most decks fail before the design: too many ideas per slide and no clear order. I start with the storyline, the 10 to 15 things the audience must remember, in the order that makes them say yes. Then each becomes a slide.
