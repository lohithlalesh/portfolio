Social media marketing in Dubai on a small-business budget

Posting every day isn't a strategy. What works for small businesses in Dubai is a clear content plan, a few formats you can repeat, and a small paid push behind the posts that are already working. That's what I set up.

What a month looks like

Social media marketing · Dubai

The monthly loop

Plan

A content calendar built around your offers, the Dubai calendar and what your audience asks about.

Make

Reels, carousels and static posts designed for each platform, plus captions and hooks.

Boost

A small, targeted paid budget behind the posts that are already performing.

Review

A short monthly report: what worked, what to cut, what to try next.

Planned around the Dubai calendar

Dubai has its own rhythm. Ramadan and Eid change when people are online and what they respond to. Dubai Shopping Festival, summer slowdowns, school terms and big events all shift attention. A good calendar plans for these instead of reacting to them.

Which platforms

  • Instagram for most consumer brands, restaurants, beauty and retail
  • TikTok for reach and younger audiences, with native-feeling video
  • LinkedIn for B2B, consultancies and founders building a personal brand
  • Snapchat and YouTube Shorts when your audience is there

Keeping it affordable

You don't need to be everywhere. Most small businesses do better with two platforms done well than five done badly. I'll recommend a scope that fits your budget, and you can start with a single month to see the work before committing to more.
