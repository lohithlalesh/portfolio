Why hire one marketer instead of an agency

Agencies in Dubai are built for big accounts. You pay for account managers, office space in a free zone tower and a team you rarely speak to. For a startup, a restaurant, a clinic, a consultancy or a growing e-commerce brand, that overhead often costs more than the work itself.

Working with me is simpler. You talk to the person who writes the copy, designs the page, edits the video and sets up the ads. There's no hand-off between departments, so things move faster and nothing gets lost in a brief.
