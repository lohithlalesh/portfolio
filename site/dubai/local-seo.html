Local SEO in Dubai: how to show up when people search near them

When someone in Dubai searches for a service near them, Google shows a map with three businesses before any website. Getting into that map is local SEO. Here's how it works, and how I can set it up for you at freelancer rates.

Lohith LaleshMarketing expert, Dubai · Updated 9 Oct 2026

What Google looks at

Google ranks local results on three things: how relevant your business is to the search, how close you are to the person searching, and how prominent you are, which includes reviews and links. You can't move your office, but you can work on the other two.

Local SEO · Dubai

The local SEO checklist

Google Business Profile

Correct category, services, hours, photos and a description that uses the words customers search.

Consistent details

The same business name, address and phone number on your site, Google and UAE directories.

Service pages

One page per service and area, written for what people in Dubai actually type.

Reviews

A simple way to ask happy customers for reviews, and replies to every one.

Mistakes I see in Dubai

  • A Google profile in the wrong category, or with no services listed
  • Different phone numbers on the website, Google and directories
  • One generic services page instead of a page per service
  • No reviews strategy, so competitors with more reviews win the map
  • A slow website on mobile, where most local searches happen
