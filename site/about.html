How I write these notes

The field notes are practical guides for businesses in Dubai and the UAE. A few rules I follow:

  • Every article answers one specific question. No templates with the location swapped.
  • Rules, figures and legal points are checked against official or reputable sources, which are listed at the end of the article with the date they were checked.
  • I use AI tools for research, outlining and drafting, the same way I do in client work. Facts are verified against the sources, not taken from the tool.
  • Nothing here is legal or financial advice. Where rules matter, I link to the authority and say so.
  • Articles are updated when rules or platforms change. If you spot something out of date, email me and I'll fix it.
